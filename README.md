## About this mod

More Swords & Weapons 9minecraft Mod brings a big collection of unique weapons to your game. It gives you new ways to fight with longswords, katanas, and magical blades that cast spells. You can find these items in treasure chests or craft them yourself to become stronger in battles. Features: \t Runic Weapons: Special swords that have powerful magic abilities built right in. \t Adds many weapon types like spears, rapiers, scythes, hammers, and twinblades. \t Each weapon has its own attack speed, damage, and reach.
